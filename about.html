THE ECOSYSTEM

Where Imagination Meets Production

We bridge classic hand-drawn frame principles with cutting-edge real-time render engines and digital storytelling techniques.

Whether it’s mastering the 12 principles of animation in 2D vector pipelines, building complex procedural shaders in Blender, or experimenting with generative audio-visual shaders in TouchDesigner, our community operates like an open, collaborative creative studio.
