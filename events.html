COMMONLY ASKED

Event & Jam Inquiries

Who can participate in FRAME SHIFT?

Open to all students across branches and colleges. Both solo animators and cross-discipline teams (animator + sound designer + editor) are welcome!

Do I need prior experience for workshops?

Not at all! Our foundational bootcamps start from the absolute basics of keyframing, timeline curves, and asset setup.

Are workshop tools free?

We emphasize open-source and student-accessible tools like Blender, Krita, DaVinci Resolve, and Spline alongside industry standards.
