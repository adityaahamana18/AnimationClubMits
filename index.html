Manifesto & Philosophy

Creativity Has No Frame.

We are a collective of digital painters, 3D worldbuilders, kinetic typographers, and meme conspirators at Madhav Institute of Technology & Science, Gwalior.

The Animation Club is a student-driven incubator where imagination meets modern creative technology. Whether you're drawing your very first keyframe, tweaking complex shader nodes in Blender, or timing high-energy video edits for social feeds, you have a room full of peers ready to collaborate.

Learn by Creating

Skip dry lectures. We build original shorts, experimental loops, and visual projects from day one.

Collaborate Across Disciplines

Pair with scriptwriters, sound designers, 3D modelers, and video editors to create unified masterpieces.

Emerging Tech & Generative

Explore Three.js web graphics, AI-assisted workflows, TouchDesigner simulations, and interactive canvas tools.

Showcase & Compete

Publish to curated club galleries, participate in national animation fests, and build industry-grade portfolios.
